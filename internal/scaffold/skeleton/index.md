# Preface

![A book cover drawn as simple shapes](images/cover.svg)

Welcome. This is the first page of your book. It comes from `index.md`.

Each chapter is a Markdown file. Their order is the `chapters` list in `octavo.yml`.

- `octavo preview` shows the book in your browser and reloads it when you save.
- `octavo render` builds the book into `_book/`.
- `octavo publish` puts it online.

Start with [the introduction](chapters/01-intro.md).
